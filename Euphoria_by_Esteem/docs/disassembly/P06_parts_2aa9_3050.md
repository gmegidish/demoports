# P06: parts 0000:2aa9 (400.40 s .. 443.45 s) and 0000:3050 (443.45 s .. 497.40 s)

Functions: 0000:2aa9 (part "glass box"), helpers 0000:2893 (shatter intro) and 0000:2484 (build the 18 shards),
box builder 0e5a:0cca; part 0000:3050 ("torus"), torus builder 0e5a:2500. Also the tiny common part prologue 0000:0000.

## Summary

**Part 2aa9** (43.0 s, all mode 13h, page 1 = back buffer, page 102 = the picture 38.pcx):
1. Palette all black and locked; load 38.pcx (a grey picture, colours 0..63, palette (i,i,i)*4 in 8-bit) into page 102.
2. 2893: the picture is shown tinted blue (one full-screen additive quad, +100, into a black-to-blue ramp at 100..163), fades in
   over 2 s, and at part time 3.00 s shatters into 18 triangles (additive +100, double-sided) that spin and fly away
   over the untinted picture until part time 6.00 s.
3. A translucent box (100x100x100, flat faces drawn with the ADDITIVE span: picture index + 64/128/192) drops from the top
   with a bouncing motion, rotates, its three colour ramps fade from blue/green/red to purple/cyan/orange,
   then it flies into the camera (T1 = 0 .. 23 s).
4. Fade to white (0.33 s), then the picture with a "wobble" (179b:060b) water distortion whose speed decays to 0 over
   11 s (and fades from white in the first 1 s), fade to white (0.5 s), white screen fading to black until part time 43.00 s.

**Part 3050** (about 52 s): a texture-mapped, Phong-ish lit torus (texture 32.pcx, orange ramp). First 8 s the camera is
inside the tube (fade-in 2 s, trails because the page is not cleared), then the torus backs away to the centre,
spins on three axes, switches to single-sided after T1 = 13 s, and from T1 = 42 s flies into the camera. A 10/11-pixel
black frame is drawn around the screen every frame. No fade-out (the torus passes the eye plane and vanishes).

**No frame pacing anywhere** (no retrace wait, no waitUntil in the loops). Part 3050 is fully time based, so any frame
rate works. Part 2aa9 has several per-iteration effects (shard velocity/rotation, box gravity bounce, the 100-step palette
fade, the wobble phase) whose real speed depends on the loop rate. From the capture (box fall of 160 units with
0.4/iteration^2 gravity in about 0.2 s; the 100-step palette fade lasting about 0.7 s, 416.5 s -> 417.2 s) the reference
machine ran the 2aa9 loops at **about 140 iterations per second** (GUESS; recommend running those loops at a fixed
140 Hz step in the port, present every display frame).

Notes:
- Rows 0..9 and 190..199 of page 1 are never rewritten during loop 2; they keep the last loop-1 frame (picture + box).

- The box colours: faces add 64 / 128 / 192 to picture indices 0..63, landing in the 64..127, 128..191, 192..255 ramps,
  so each face shows the picture tinted in that ramp's colour. Opposite faces have the same colour (see BoxInit).
- Screen y grows downward (screen = y + 100), so A = -160 starts the box above the screen.
- The wobble phase bytes keep running with speed v; at v = 0 the picture stays displaced (the displacement is never 0).
- Checked against the capture: 400.4-403.3 blue picture fading in; 403.4 shards; 404-406.5 plain picture;
  406.5 box drops from the top; red/green/blue faces until about 416.5, purple/cyan/orange from about 417.2;
  about 428-429.5 the box fills the screen; 429.6 white; 431-440 slightly wavy picture; 441.5 white; then grey to black.
